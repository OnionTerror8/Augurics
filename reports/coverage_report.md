# Coverage Diagnostic — Protocol P1, E1

Run BEFORE any model fit, per PROTOCOL_P1.md. Month-by-month price and event coverage and mean sales, overall and per category, on the full ingested M5 panel — no filtering applied yet (that is task 1.4).

## Finding: sustained low price coverage in early years (label-integrity decision)

Aggregate (volume-pooled) `price_coverage` does not reach and hold 90% until **2014-04**. Below that threshold, more than 10% of panel rows have no `sell_price` — these are structurally not-yet-introduced (product, store) pairs logging zero sales, not true absence-of-demand signal. This is a multi-year ramp (the M5 assortment grows steadily from 2011 to 2016), not a single-month cliff, but it is the same label-integrity concern the diagnostic exists to catch: early-period mean_sales and price-covariate availability are not comparable to later periods.

**Decision:** any pooled/aggregate view of the panel (comparator fits, category-level summaries) bounds its training window to **2014-04 onward** for price-covariate reliability. This is independent of, and does not replace, the per-series active-span filter in task 1.4, which removes individual not-yet-introduced series regardless of calendar date.
